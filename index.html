Frequently asked questions

How do I connect Four Corner to Claude or ChatGPT? +

Four Corner uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard. When your access is approved, simply add our server address and your login credentials into Claude Desktop or ChatGPT. Your AI assistant will immediately be able to search verified property listings directly in your chat without any browser extensions.

Where does the property data come from, and how is it verified? +

Unlike regular property websites that are filled with duplicate broker ads, Four Corner gathers details directly from official RERA documents, approved architectural floor plans, and official builder price sheets. Room sizes show true carpet area rather than inflated numbers, and prices show the complete cost with no broker fees.

Will my phone number be shared, or will brokers call me? +

Never. We do not sell your contact details to telemarketers or share your information with third-party brokers. Your searches are completely private in your AI conversation. If you decide to visit or contact a project, you deal directly with the builder's sales office.

How are rush-hour commute times calculated? +

We do not rely on sales claims like "5 minutes from Gachibowli." Instead, we calculate commute times using real weekday rush-hour traffic (8:30 AM – 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM) to key work hubs including Financial District, Kokapet, Gachibowli, and Hitec City.

Why use an AI assistant with MCP instead of a traditional website? +

Traditional property websites use rigid filters and promote paid ads from brokers. They cannot easily answer questions about sunlight direction, floor layouts, bedroom dimensions, or actual traffic. By connecting your AI to Four Corner, you can ask in plain words and get clear, objective answers from verified builder records.
